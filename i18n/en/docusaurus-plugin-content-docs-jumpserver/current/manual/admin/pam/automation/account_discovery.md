---
title: Account Discovery
---

## 1 Overview
:::note

- Click the **PAM** button on the navigation bar to open the **PAM** page.
- Click **Automation &gt; Account Discovery** to open the **Account Discovery** page.
- The account discovery feature can collect account information from assets managed by JumpServer. By executing tasks, you can collect asset account information. JumpServer supports binding discovered account information to managed assets, reducing manual operations.
:::
## 2 Discover accounts
:::note

- After selecting accounts, click **Sync Selected** in the **More** menu to bind collected users to corresponding assets.
:::
![automation_01](/img/jumpserver/v4_account_discovery_01.png)
:::note

- Click **Delete Synced Selection** in the operations to delete accounts from the server.
:::
![automation_02](/img/jumpserver/v4_account_discovery_02.png)
:::note

- Click **Delete Selected** in the operations to delete accounts from account discovery.
:::
![automation_03](/img/jumpserver/v4_account_discovery_03.png)
:::note

- After account binding, the account source will be discovered.
:::
![automation_04](/img/jumpserver/v4_account_discovery_04.png)
## 3 Account discovery task
:::note

- Click the **Create** button on the **Account Discovery Task** page to create a user discovery task.
:::
![automation_05](/img/jumpserver/v4_account_discovery_05.png)
:::note

- After successful creation, select the **Execute** button to execute the user collection task.
:::
![automation_06](/img/jumpserver/v4_account_discovery_06.png)
## 4 Execution history
:::note

- The execution list page mainly displays detailed information and logs of executed user discovery tasks. Click the **Logs** button or **Report** button next to the corresponding executed log to view detailed task execution information.
:::
![automation_07](/img/jumpserver/v4_account_discovery_07.png)
